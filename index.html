→ Verbs
→ → Ser

When to Use the Spanish Verb "Ser"

A simple way to remember when to use the Spanish verb "ser."

  • Name - I'm Erlin / Soy Erlin
  • Identity - He is Elkin / Él es Elkin
  • Professions - I'm a student / Soy estudiante
  • Nationalities - I'm Mexican / Soy mexicano
  • Countries of origin - I'm from Spain / Soy de España
  • Relationships - I'm his father / Soy su padre
  • People - He is a man / Él es un hombre
  • Physical Attributes - He is tall / Él es alto
  • Personality Traits - I'm happy / Soy alegre
  • Characteristic (Using Home) - The house is big / La casa es grande
  • Dates - Is it March 4th? / ¿Es cuatro de marzo?
  • Events - When is your birthday? / ¿Cuándo es tu cumpleaños?
→ → Llamarse

When to Use the Spanish Verb "Llamarse"

A simple way to remember when to use the Spanish verb "llamarse."

  • Introducing yourself or name - My name is John / Me llamo John
  • Identity - His name is Elkin / Él se llama Elkin
→ → Tener

When to Use the Spanish Verb "Tener"

A simple way to remember when to use the Spanish verb "tener."

  • Relationships - I have a brother / Tengo un hermano
  • Specific body features - He has light eyes / Él tiene ojos claros
  • Possession (Rooms) - I have a small apartment / Tengo un apartamento pequeño
→ Adverbs

Question

a sentence or expression used to ask for information, an answer, or clarification.

  • What is your brother like? = ¿Cómo es tu hermano?
  • Where is your house? = ¿Dónde está tu casa?
  • What is a computer? = ¿Qué es una computadora?

Expression

a word, phrase, or group of words used to communicate an idea, feeling, thought, or response.

  • Yes, I am a student / Sí, soy estudiante
  • No, I don't have a dog / No, no tengo un perro

What do you do?
